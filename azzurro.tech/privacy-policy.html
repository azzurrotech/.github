Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Azzurro Technology Inc. (“Azzurro”, “we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information we collect in the course of providing our services. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, how long we retain it, and the rights you have under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and other applicable regulations.

By visiting https://azzurro.tech, using our web-based portals, or engaging us for any of the services listed above, you consent to the practices described herein. If you do not agree, please do not use our website or services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provision of Services – Design, develop, deliver, and support the software, hardware, and data-management solutions you have contracted.
  • Account Management – Create, authenticate, and maintain your user account; enforce security policies and warranty periods (max 12 months from the last valid payment).
  • Communication – Send order confirmations, invoices, quotes, status updates, warranty notifications, and marketing communications (only if you have opted-in).
  • Support & Warranty – Diagnose issues, provide patches, security updates, and other support activities covered by the warranty.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance – Retain records for tax, accounting, anti-fraud, and lawful request compliance.
  • Improvement & Analytics – Analyze usage patterns (via aggregated, non-identifiable data) to improve our products and website.
  • Third-Party Services – Facilitate payment processing, email delivery, spam detection, and optional embedded media.

6. International Data Transfers

All primary processing occurs on servers located in Canada. If a third-party service processes data outside Canada (e.g., a CDN), we ensure that the transfer is protected by standard contractual clauses or that the provider adheres to privacy standards comparable to Canadian law.

8. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy statutes, you have the following rights (subject to lawful exceptions):

  • Access – Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction – Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion – Request erasure of your personal data (except where we must retain it for legal, tax, or security purposes).
  • Portability – Receive your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format.
  • Objection – Object to direct marketing communications (you can unsubscribe at any time).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe (generally within 30 days).

9. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard safeguards, including:

  • Encryption – TLS 1.3 for data in transit; AES-256 encryption for data at rest.
  • Access Controls – Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication for staff.
  • Monitoring & Auditing – Continuous intrusion detection, regular security audits, and vulnerability assessments.
  • Physical Security – Data centres in Canada with restricted access, CCTV, and environmental controls.

While we strive to protect your information, no transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; you acknowledge the inherent risks of online communication.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory changes, new services, or operational adjustments. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Post the revised policy on https://azzurro.tech with a new “Last updated” date.
  • Notify existing customers via email (if you have provided an email address).

Your continued use of our website or services after such notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
