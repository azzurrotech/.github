Who We Are

Based in Canada, Azzurro Technology blends deep expertise in open-source software and cutting-edge data sovereignty solutions. Our mission is simple: give organizations certainty that with open source based software solutions, we can get their software problem solved.

What We Do

1

Integrated Open-Source Software Solutions

From concept to production, we design, develop, and deploy end-to-end open-source solutions tailored to your business needs. Every solution ships with a comprehensive warranty, guaranteeing performance, security patches, and ongoing support for the lifespan of the deployment. Whether you need a micro-service architecture, a real-time analytics platform, or a bespoke workflow engine, we deliver robust, auditable code you can trust. Contact our team through the form below to get started!

2

Ensure Your Solution Meets Your Requirements

We provide assurance that any given solution meets all your organizational requirements. This does not simply include use cases, but legislative requirements (GDPR, PIPEDA), industry standards and security standards from industry and internally to your organization. We will report on any gaps and offer clear recommendations which can be immediately implemented to solve the identified issues. If you have concerns during the warranty period, we will address them immediately or offer a full refund of any payments.

3

Complementary Advice and a Catalogue of Exceptional Vendors

We always offer free advice and publish our blog with various content related to security, open source software and other similar topics. We have our catalogue which includes offers from various software development companies, SaaS vendors, open source projects and cloud providers who recognize the important role that open source software plays in a modern technical stack. We do offer paid support services for different advice solutions, but these services are never billed hourly, only based on outcome.
